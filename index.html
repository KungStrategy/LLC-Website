Christopher Anderson LLC

Christopher Anderson LLC is a registered limited liability corporation with the state of Michigan, USA. It is 100% owned by Christopher Anderson and he uses it for any and all business endeavors. Currently it's only business endeavor is the launching of an app called 50/50 Commercials.

50/50 Commercials is an app that shows people commercials and allows them to collect 50% of the commercials value. Payments are thru PayPal and people can cash out at the nearest cent earned. There is also an economic incentive if the person gets a friend to download the app and watch the first commercial.
